Lab sign-up, the weekly schedule and all the lab assignments live on our course site. You book your own seat.

Book lab time

How to book

  1. Pick your course (MTE200 or MTE210) and the lab you are taking.
  2. Enter your name and email, then hit + Take seat on a free seat.
  3. Put the session in your calendar with Add to calendar.

Worth knowing

  • You take each lab once — a second seat in the same lab is refused.
  • You cannot book two labs in the same time slot.
  • Can't make it? Cancel. The seat frees up for someone else immediately.
  • Booked on another computer or phone? Enter your email under Booked on another device? to find your bookings again.
  • MTE200 runs Tuesdays (09:00–11:30 and 11:30–14:00), MTE210 Wednesdays (10:15–13:00).
  • Every lab runs in KE E-455, the Medical Technology Lab.

Approval

There is no written hand-in. You present your results orally to the lab engineer in the lab and are approved there and then. Every lab assignment ends with an Approval section listing exactly what you need to be able to show and explain.

Your name and email are stored only to run the lab sign-up, and are visible to course staff. Other students see your first name on the schedule. Bookings are deleted once the semester is over — at the earliest 30 days after the last lab day — while the record that the lab was approved is kept. Ask course staff if you want your booking deleted sooner.
